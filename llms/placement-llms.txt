# In-App Routes — Authentication Required

> Everything below this line requires a signed-in Exec session. A public
> or anonymous agent fetching one of these paths will get a login
> redirect, not the content described — do not attempt to fetch them
> without valid session credentials. This section exists for the in-app
> onboarding agent to navigate a signed-in user's own workspace.

## Coaching

- [Coach Profile](/coaches/:coachSlug): Public coach profile with bio, specialties, services, and booking options.
- [Coach Specialty Profile](/coaches/:coachSlug/:specialtySlug): Coach profile filtered to one specialty, with related services and booking.
- [Select Coach](/schedule): Pick a coach before booking a one-on-one coaching session.
- [Schedule Session](/schedule/:coachId): Choose a time slot and book a session with the selected coach.
- [Coach Matching](/match): Start coach matching by sharing goals and preferences.
- [Choose Coach](/choose): Review recommended coaches and select who to work with.
- [Coaching](/coaching): Browse coaches, view upcoming and past sessions, and schedule new coaching.
- [Sessions](/sessions): List upcoming and past coaching sessions with scheduling details.
- [Session Details](/sessions/:meetingId): Session detail with meeting info, notes, and follow-up actions.
- [Messages](/messages): Inbox for direct messages with coaches, clients.

## Certifications

- [Public Credential](/credentials/:uuid): Login-free page for one issued certificate: recipient, issuer, dates, status and credential ID. Linked from LinkedIn.

- [Create Certification](/certifications/create): Create a certification with description and expiration details.
- [Certification Reports](/certifications/reports): Workspace-wide certification completion and progress reporting.

- [Edit Certification](/certifications/:id/edit): Edit certification details, requirements, and learner assignments.
- [My Certificates](/certificates): Your earned certificates with status, dates, and LinkedIn share actions.
- [Certificate Download](/certificates/:id): View or download a certificate a learner has earned.
